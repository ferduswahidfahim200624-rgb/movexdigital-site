# MoveX Digital — Cold Outreach Specialist Skill

Use this skill whenever drafting cold DMs (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook) or cold emails to prospect B2B, eCommerce, or local service businesses for **MoveX Digital**.

---

## 1. Strict Golden Rules of MoveX Cold Outreach
1. **Strict Word Count (50–70 Words Maximum):** Prospects read outreach on their phones in under 5 seconds. Every word must earn its place.
2. **ZERO Links in the First Message:** Never include `movexdigital.site`, Calendly links, or portfolio URLs in Message #1. Links trigger spam filters and make the message look like an automated blast.
3. **Value-First Observation (No Generic Fluff):** Skip *"I hope this finds you well"* or *"We are a full-service agency with 9+ years of experience."* Open directly with a specific observation about **their** business.
4. **Focus on 1 of 3 High-Impact Revenue Gaps:**
   - **Angle A — Google Ads / Meta Tracking Gap:** Missing GA4/GTM events, double-counted conversions, or lack of Stape Server-Side / Meta CAPI causing Smart Bidding to waste 25–35% of budget.
   - **Angle B — Website UX & Conversion Rate (CRO) Gap:** Slow mobile load, buried call-to-action above the fold, or friction in the booking/checkout funnel leaking paid traffic.
   - **Angle C — AI Search (GEO) & Local SEO Visibility Gap:** Competitors appearing in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, or the Top 3 Map Pack for high-intent buyer queries while their brand is invisible.
5. **Permission-Based Micro-CTA:** Ask for permission to send a 90-second video/screenshot breakdown—never ask for a 30-minute sales call in the first message.

## 2. Proven 50–70 Word Outreach Frameworks

### Angle A: Google Ads & Conversion Tracking Gap (58 Words)
> Hi {{FirstName}}, I came across {{CompanyName}}'s Google Ads while researching {{Niche}} in {{City}}.  
>  
> I noticed your landing page fires a basic page-view tag, but enhanced conversion and server-side signals aren't passing back to Google—which usually inflates CPA by 25–35%.  
>  
> We recently cut a similar campaign's CPA by 67%. Mind if I send a 60-second screenshot showing the exact fix?

### Angle B: Website UX & Funnel Conversion Gap (56 Words)
> Hi {{FirstName}}, checked out {{CompanyName}}'s website from your {{Platform}} profile—love how clean your {{Service/Product}} positioning is.  
>  
> On mobile, though, the primary booking/quote flow takes 3 extra taps below the fold, which typically drops paid traffic conversions by 20–30%.  
>  
> I mocked up a quick 3-point UX tweak to plug that leak. Open to me sharing it here?

### Angle C: AI Search (GEO) & SEO Visibility Gap (61 Words)
> Hi {{FirstName}}, when prospects ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews for the best {{Niche}} in {{City}}, {{CompetitorName}} is currently being cited first while {{CompanyName}} isn't indexed for AI search entities yet.  
>  
> We help brands capture both traditional SEO and AI Search leads. Mind if I send a quick 1-page snapshot showing the 3 signals you're missing?
