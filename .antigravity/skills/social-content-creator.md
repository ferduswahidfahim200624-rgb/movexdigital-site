# MoveX Digital — Social Content Creator Skill

Use this skill whenever creating, editing, or scheduling social media content for **MoveX Digital (`movexdigital.site`)** across LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook.

---

## 1. Audience & Language Adaptation
- **International B2B & Local Service Businesses (US, UK, Australia, Canada, Germany, UAE):**
  - Write in **100% crisp, natural, professional English**.
  - Focus on ROI, qualified lead flow, CPA reduction, server-side tracking accuracy, and AI Search (GEO) visibility.
- **Local Market Clients (Bangladesh):**
  - Use a **natural Bengali + English mix** (conversational Bangla paired with standard English marketing/technical terms like *Google Ads, Conversion Tracking, ROAS, Landing Page, Qualified Leads*).
  - Keep it relatable, practical, and authority-building.

---

## 2. Core Brand Voice & Formatting Rules
- **Tone:** Confident, clear, friendly, practical, outcome-focused.
- **Sentence Structure:** Short, punchy sentences. Leave generous white space (1–2 sentences per paragraph) for mobile readability.
- **Proof Over Promises:** Never say *"We are the #1 agency."* Instead say *"How we cut CPA by 67% and tracked 282 verified calls/forms in 30 days using GTM + Stape Server-Side."*
- **Human + AI Positioning:** Highlight how MoveX Digital combines **9+ years of human campaign mastery (500+ clients, $6M+ spend)** with **practical AI & automation workflows**.

---

## 3. The 4-Part High-Converting Post Structure

### Part 1: The Pattern-Interrupt Hook (Lines 1–2)
Grab attention immediately by calling out a costly problem, a counter-intuitive insight, or a concrete result:
- *"Your ads aren't broken. Your conversion tracking is."*
- *"Most businesses lose 25–40% of their ad attribution before Smart Bidding even optimizes."*
- *"How a local service business went from $83.88 CPA down to $27.87 CPA in 14 days."*

### Part 2: The Diagnostic Breakdown (The "Why")
Explain the problem in plain business language (no confusing jargon):
- Show why vanity clicks or broken GA4/GTM tags drain budget.
- Contrast the **Typical Broken Setup** vs. **MoveX Digital's Outcome-First Fix**.

### Part 3: Proof-Based Actionable Points (3–5 Bullets)
Give the reader real value they can trust:
- Reference real tools from our stack (`Google Ads`, `Meta Ads`, `GA4`, `GTM`, `Stape.io`, `Semrush`, `Search Console`, `WordPress`, `Shopify`, `GoHighLevel`).
- Share specific levers: Negative keyword shields, server-side event deduplication, AI Search / GEO entity optimization, or high-converting landing page UX.

### Part 4: Low-Friction Call to Action (CTA)
End with a clear, value-driven next step:
- **Audit CTA:** *"Want us to inspect your Google Ads, Tracking, or SEO/GEO visibility? DM me **'AUDIT'** or book a free diagnostic at `movexdigital.site`."*
