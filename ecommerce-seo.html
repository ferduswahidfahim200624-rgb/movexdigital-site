COMMON QUESTIONS

Know what you are choosing.

Should every product page be optimized?

No. Prioritize based on demand, value, competition, and catalogue structure.

Can SEO fix low conversion alone?

No. Checkout, pricing, stock, product experience, and trust also affect sales.

Do you work with Shopify or WooCommerce?

We can plan and coordinate improvements; implementation depends on theme, apps, and access.

How do you handle unavailable products?

Treatment depends on whether a product is temporarily unavailable, discontinued, or replaced.
