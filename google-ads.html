GOOGLE ADS PACKAGE FAQ

Know what you are choosing.

We will confirm the practical details during scoping, before the work starts.

Are the packages fixed price?

No. We scope the fee after reviewing the campaign mix, account complexity, tracking requirements, and support cadence. That keeps the recommendation relevant to the actual work.

Is my advertising budget included?

No. The management fee and media spend are separate. You pay Google directly for advertising, with your account and billing remaining under your business.

Can I start with an audit and decide later?

Yes. An audit and action plan can be a standalone engagement. You can use its priorities internally or ask MoveX to implement and manage the agreed work.

What if my tracking is not set up correctly?

We review the current measurement first and identify what needs correction. Tracking setup or coordination can be included in scope, depending on your website, analytics, consent setup, and access.

How long before results are clear?

Timing depends on competition, budget, conversion volume, and the state of the account and website. We use early data to identify issues, then allow enough relevant conversion evidence before drawing conclusions about performance.

Do you guarantee leads or a specific ROAS?

No. Ad platforms, market demand, pricing, competition, and the website all affect outcomes. We commit to the agreed work, clear measurement, and evidence-led optimization—not a guaranteed result.
